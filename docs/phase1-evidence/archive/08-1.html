An antidetect browser (also called anti fingerprint browser) is an anonymous browser designed to minimize the ability of websites and tracking systems to identify and track your digital fingerprint. These undetected browsers employ various techniques to mask or alter browser fingerprints. After fingerprint spoofing, you can act like a real person to make it difficult for websites or online platforms to identify and keep your multiple accounts secure.

AdsPower antidetect browser allows you to create multiple profiles with unique browser fingerprints based on Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS.
